About

About SavorCline

SavorCline was founded in 2026 to help food manufacturers see allergen cross-contact risk in the production schedule, before a shift starts, instead of finding it on a failed swab or a recall notice.

Mission

Make every allergen changeover visible, checked and signed off before production begins.

How we build

With early-access QA teams, on their own schedules, with every finding traceable to a record.

Team

The Team Behind SavorCline

Roadmap

Where SavorCline is going

Built with early-access QA teams. The order is our plan and may change based on what pilots teach us.

  1. Now

    Live demo & early access

    • Rule-based risk engine, sequencing and cleaning checks
    • Interactive live demo on a sample bakery
    • Proposed AWS architecture defined
    • Early-access requests open
  2. Next

    Pilot on one site

    • Your own products, lines and cleaning records
    • Document upload with Amazon Textract
    • Bedrock-assisted allergen extraction with human review
  3. Then

    General availability

    • Multi-user workspaces with Amazon Cognito roles
    • Server-side activity log and report archive
    • Pricing confirmed with pilot sites
  4. Later

    Exploring with pilot sites

    • ERP / MES schedule import
    • Audit pack templates for BRCGS and SQF sites
    • Multi-site views for groups and consultants
