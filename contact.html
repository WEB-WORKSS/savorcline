FAQ

Questions QA teams ask first.

Email the founder
Is SavorCline available now?

SavorCline is building its initial product. The live demo on this site is fully interactive and runs on sample data or data you enter, stored only in your browser. Document upload and the AWS-hosted platform are in development, and early-access requests are open.

Does SavorCline replace our HACCP plan or allergen risk assessment?

No. It applies your allergen data and cleaning rules to the schedule consistently and shows where they are not met. Your HACCP team still owns the controls, the validation of cleaning methods and the final decision on every run.

What does the AI do?

In the planned platform, Amazon Textract reads supplier documents and Amazon Bedrock suggests allergen profiles and explains findings in plain language. A person confirms every extracted allergen. Risk levels come from deterministic rules, not from the AI.

Does the AI decide the production order?

No. SavorCline suggests a run order that needs fewer allergen cleans. A planner or QA lead accepts or rejects it.

Who can change what?

Planners edit schedules, QA confirms allergen profiles and signs off reports, and auditors get read-only access. In the demo roles are not enforced; in production they are enforced on the server.

Which allergens are covered?

The demo uses the 14 allergens regulated under UK and EU food information law, including hidden sources such as whey (milk), spelt (gluten) and tahini (sesame). The list is configurable for other markets.

How much does it cost?

Proposed launch pricing starts at $249 a month per site. Final pricing is agreed with pilot sites, and no payment is taken today. See the Pricing page.

What data do we need to start?

A product list with allergen information (or the supplier specifications to extract it from), a production schedule per line, and your cleaning records for the same period.
