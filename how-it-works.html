How it works

From supplier spec to a checked schedule in four steps.

SavorCline works from the documents your site already keeps. People confirm every allergen profile and approve every run order.

How it works

Start with documents you already have.

No sensors, no factory hardware, no MES integration to begin with. SavorCline works from specifications, schedules and cleaning logs, and people make the final call.

  1. 01

    Add products and specs

    Upload or paste supplier specifications and recipe sheets. Allergens are extracted, including hidden sources such as whey, spelt or tahini, and shown for review.

  2. 02

    Confirm allergen profiles

    A QA team member confirms each product's "contains" and "may contain" allergens. Confirmed profiles become the reference for every check.

  3. 03

    Check the week's schedule

    Every changeover on every line is assessed against the cleaning records. Risky transitions are ranked high, medium or controlled.

  4. 04

    Approve and report

    Accept or reject the suggested run order, then generate a dated report with findings, required cleans and a sign-off block.

What goes in

  • Supplier specifications and recipe sheets
  • Your product list and each product's allergens
  • The production schedule for each line
  • Cleaning records: type, time, sign-off and swab result

What comes out

  • A confirmed allergen profile for every product
  • A risk rating for every changeover, with the reason
  • Cleaning gaps: missing, wrong type, unsigned or unswabbed
  • A suggested run order and a dated report

What your team does

  • Confirms extracted allergens before they are used
  • Accepts or rejects each suggested run order
  • Fixes gaps before the shift and signs off the report

AI, used carefully

The rules decide. The AI reads documents and explains.

Risk findings come from deterministic rules you can trace. AI is used to read supplier documents and to explain findings in plain language, and a person confirms everything before it is used.

What the AI does

  • Reads ingredient lists and specifications, including scanned PDFs (planned, with Amazon Textract)
  • Maps ingredients such as whey, casein or spelt to the allergen they contain
  • Explains a finding in plain language and lists the records it used
  • Suggests allergen profiles for a QA person to confirm

What it never does

  • Approve a run order or sign off a clean
  • Decide the risk level: the rules do, and show their working
  • Fill in a missing cleaning record or swab result
  • Use one customer's documents to answer another's questions
