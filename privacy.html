Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information SavorCline collects through savorcline.site and the SavorCline live demo, why, and the choices you have. SavorCline is building its initial product, so this policy describes how the website and demo work today and will be updated before customer accounts open.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: when you use the early-access form or email us, we receive your name, work email, company, role (if given), company size, plan of interest, primary use case, your message and your consent to be contacted.
  • Live demo data: the demo has no accounts. Products, schedules and cleaning records you enter are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent to us.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider may log standard request data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.

How we use information

  • To reply to inquiries and early-access requests
  • To operate, maintain and improve the website and product
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use form details for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Our website host, to serve this site
  • Google Fonts, for web fonts loaded from Google's servers
  • Your email provider, when you choose to contact us by email
  • Amazon Web Services, for the planned product infrastructure (not used to process personal information today)

Retention

We keep inquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them on request. Retention periods for account data will be added before customer accounts open.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it: email bello@savorcline.site. You can clear live-demo data at any time with "Start empty" in the demo or by clearing your browser's site data. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to personal information to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and change the date above.
