Security

Security and safeguards

How SavorCline is designed to protect supplier documents, keep people in control and keep every finding traceable, and what is and isn't built yet.

What the demo does with data

  • The live demo runs entirely in your browser.
  • Products, schedules and cleaning records you enter are stored in your browser's local storage only and never sent to a server.
  • You can reload the sample or start empty at any time.

How production will handle your documents

  • Supplier specifications, schedules and cleaning records are stored on AWS, partitioned by customer.
  • Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest with per-tenant keys.
  • Hosting regions are agreed with each pilot site.

Access and roles

  • Users sign in with Amazon Cognito; there are no shared logins.
  • Planners, QA and auditors each get only the actions their role needs.
  • Roles are enforced on the server, not only in the interface.

How the AI is constrained

  • Risk ratings come from deterministic rules that show their working.
  • AI only reads documents, suggests allergen profiles and explains findings.
  • Every AI-extracted allergen is confirmed by a person before it is used.

Audit trail

  • Profile confirmations, schedule changes, approvals and reports are written to an activity log.
  • Each entry records who acted and when.

What isn't built yet

  • The AWS document pipeline, sign-in and server-side roles are proposed, not deployed.
  • No certifications are claimed. We will not describe any as held until they are.

Safeguards

Built for QA teams that have to sign the report.

Every finding is traceable

Each risk shows the two runs, the allergens left on the line and the cleaning record it was checked against.

People make the call

Suggested run orders and AI-extracted allergens are reviewed and approved by a person before they are used.

Missing is never 'fine'

A missing cleaning record, sign-off or swab is flagged as a gap, never treated as a pass.

Supplier data kept separate

The architecture isolates each customer's documents and encrypts them with per-tenant keys.

Roles, enforced

Planners, QA and auditors each get only the actions they need. Production enforces roles on the server.

Everything recorded

Profile confirmations, schedule changes, approvals and reports are written to an activity log.
