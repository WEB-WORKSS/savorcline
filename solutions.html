Built for food manufacturers with shared lines.

Wherever several allergens share ovens, depositors, mixers or packing lines, the changeover is where cross-contact happens. SavorCline checks every one.

Solutions

Built first for bakery and confectionery.

Shared ovens, depositors and enrobers, five or more allergens in rotation, and several changeovers per shift make bakery and confectionery the hardest case. Patterns proven there carry over to other categories.

Used by: Technical / Quality Manager · Food Safety (HACCP) Team Lead · Production Planner · Operations Director

  • Bakery & confectionery

    Wheat, milk, egg, nuts and sesame on shared ovens and depositors, with frequent changeovers.

    How they use SavorCline: Check each day's oven and depositor schedule, move nut and sesame runs to the end of the day, and confirm an allergen wet clean and swab before every gluten-only run.

    First focus

  • Dairy processing

    Milk-based runs alongside plant-based or inclusion products that add nuts, soya or gluten.

    How they use SavorCline: Keep plant-based and inclusion products from following milk-based runs without a verified clean, and catch 'may contain' labels that the schedule makes necessary.

  • Packaged & ready meals

    Many SKUs, many sub-ingredients, many supplier specifications to keep current.

    How they use SavorCline: Build allergen profiles from many supplier specifications at once and re-check the week's schedule whenever a recipe or supplier changes.

  • Meat processing

    Marinades, coatings and fillers that bring mustard, celery, gluten and sulphites onto the line.

    How they use SavorCline: Track mustard, celery, gluten and sulphites brought in by marinades and coatings, and order runs so those lines need fewer full cleans.

  • Food safety & QA consultants

    Review allergen controls across several client sites from one workspace.

    How they use SavorCline: Review several client sites' schedules and cleaning records from one workspace and hand each client a dated report.
